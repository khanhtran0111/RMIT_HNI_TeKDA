# RMIT Hackathon 2026

Our team's work for the RMIT GenAI & Cybersecurity Hackathon 2026.

**Result: Top 3 in Hanoi and Top 12 overall**, competing alongside talented teams from Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Malaysia and Australia.

## Challenges

| Folder | Challenge | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| [C1](C1/README.md) | Multilingual prompt-attack classification ([Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rmit-hackathon-2026)) | Classify prompts in Portuguese, French, Italian and English as `BENIGN`, `JAILBREAK` or `PROMPT_INJECTION`, scored by macro-F1. Our XLM-RoBERTa-large + TF-IDF ensemble reached 0.971 out-of-fold macro-F1. |
| [C2](C2/README.md) | Heirloom, built with TideCloak | A digital-inheritance vault: secrets are encrypted in the owner's browser and released to each chosen recipient only after independent trusted people approve, so no single party (including us) can read or release them. [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hqr1gF16Kys). |
